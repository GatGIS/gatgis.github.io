Raider Class Guide

How classes play in the fight, what healer does each turn, and what loot each class is best at finding.

Core Stat Meanings

  • HP: Max health. If this reaches 0, the raider is down.
  • ATK: Base outgoing damage.
  • DEF: Damage reduction in damage formula.
  • SPD: Speed stat used in scaling and fight setup.
  • CRIT %: Chance to critically strike and boost output.
  • CRIT DMG: Critical damage/heal multiplier strength.
  • Rage: Extra damage multiplier for DPS-style hits.
  • Thorns: Reflect damage when hit.
  • Regen: Self-heal after own action.
  • Heal: Healer's direct healing power.

Class Roles and Base Stats

  • DPS: HP 650, ATK 105, DEF 45, SPD 1.10, CRIT 13%, CRIT DMG 150, Rage 25. Best raw damage and crit pressure.
  • Tank: HP 780, ATK 80, DEF 60, SPD 0.95, CRIT 6%, CRIT DMG 130, Thorns 25. Soaks hits, punishes boss with thorns, and is more likely to be targeted by basic boss attacks.
  • Healer: HP 620, ATK 75, DEF 45, SPD 1.00, CRIT 8%, CRIT DMG 140, Heal 50. Hybrid support that still attacks.

Important Fight Rules

  • Tank aggro tendency: Boss basic targeting is weighted to tanks (about 70% tank / 30% non-tank when possible).
  • Thorns retaliation: If you hit a raider with Thorns, the attacker takes reflected damage immediately.
  • Regen timing: Regen triggers after that raider acts. At 20% HP or lower, regen heals double.
  • Boss enrage: When boss HP drops to 20% or less, boss damage increases by 80%.
  • One-shot protection: A full-HP unit cannot be dropped from full to 0 in one hit; it survives at 1 HP.
  • Loot balance rule: Boss gets stronger loot multipliers than raiders to reduce the 6-raider snowball effect.

Healer Turn Logic (Exact)

  • When everyone is alive: Healer attacks every turn. Every second healer turn, it also heals the lowest-HP-ratio ally.
  • If any ally is down: Healer enters a 3-step loop: heal, heal, then revive.
  • Revive timing: The revive happens on every 3rd healer action while at least one ally is down.
  • Revive amount: Revived ally returns at 25% of their max HP.
  • Heal target rule: Heals the ally with the lowest HP ratio.
  • Repeat-heal penalty: If healer heals the same target as last healer turn, heal amount is reduced to 75%.
  • Critical heal rule: If target is at or below 30% HP, healer can critically heal based on healer CRIT % and CRIT DMG.

Class Loot Bias and Specials

  • DPS loot bias: More Weapons and Cups. Weapon drops can roll extra Rage (25% chance).
  • Tank loot bias: More Armor and Helmets. Armor has 60% chance and Helmet has 50% chance to roll Thorns plus Regen.
  • Healer loot bias: More Cups and Consumables. Healer can get Heal Potions that add healing power and Regen.
  • Universal categories: Weapon, Armor, Helmet, Cup. Healer pool also includes Consumable by design.
